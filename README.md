# Neon Underworld news feed

`news.json` is the news feed shown in Neon Underworld, an unofficial fan-made
companion app. A scheduled job refreshes it every two hours.

`facts.json` holds short, confirmed facts about the released game, gathered
from published coverage, that the app's AI assistants answer from. A
scheduled job refreshes it twice a day. Each fact links to its source.

Headlines and links belong to their publishers. Neon Underworld is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two
Interactive.
